# 🏢 Manhattan Luxury Penthouse RWA Tokenization (`ManhattanRWA_v3.sol`)

A production-grade, highly secure **Real World Asset (RWA)** tokenization framework custom-built for the **SimpleChain Network**. This infrastructure fractionalizes a luxury Manhattan penthouse valued at **$15,000,000 USD** into **10,000 liquid governance shares**.

## 🎯 Architectural Update (v3 Engineering Ledger)
The previous version (`v2`) generated by standard automated AI sequences contained several fatal vulnerabilities. Version 3 completely refactors the framework to architectural compliance standards:
1. **Eliminated Capital Lockup**: Added the `withdrawFundingCapital` gateway, allowing the real estate issuer to legally extract the $15M crowdfunding pool upon 100% tokenization.
2. **Fixed Integer Truncation**: Replaced the faulty division model with a high-precision multiplier (`SCALE_FACTOR = 1e18`) to ensure monthly rental yield distribution scales accurately down to a single Wei without mathematical rounding leaks.
3. **Isolated Buyback Metrics**: Completely uncoupled the asset de-tokenization tracking logic from `address(this).balance` to mitigate liquidity pool mixing risks.
4. **Secured Value Transfers**: Replaced the obsolete `.transfer()` method with low-level `.call` patterns to guarantee native gas compatibility for institutional investors using multi-signature frameworks (Gnosis Safe, DAO vaults).

## 📉 Economic Assumptions: Native Coin vs. Stablecoins

**Testnet Architecture Note**: In production environments, real-world assets are heavily tied to fiat-pegged stablecoins (USDC/USDT) or decentralized price oracles (Chainlink) to safeguard capital from crypto market volatility.

For the **SimpleChain Testnet execution layer**, this contract intentionally utilizes a **Native Proxy Model** ($1 \text{ SFT Share} = 1 \text{ ETH}$). This eliminates the operational overhead of third-party ERC-20 dependency management while testing the core smart contract primitives. The system assumes a synthetic fixed valuation matrix where 10,000 ETH matches the baseline asset purchase and reverse-buyback requirements ($15,000,000 USD total cap).

## 🛠️ Core System Features

### 1. Crowdfunding & Refund Guarantees (`purchasePropertyShares`)
* **Mechanism**: Public or institutional accounts mint `SFT` tokens inside a strict funding window.
* **Emergency Safety Valves**: If funding targets stall or are cancelled by management, `cancelFunding` triggers an immutable state transition to `FundingFailed`, permanently enabling 100% principal capital retrieval via `claimRefund`.

### 2. Secondary OTC Market Infrastructure (`transferShares`)
* **Mechanism**: Enables permissionless, peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers of asset fractions between external wallets.
* **Financial Sync Guard**: Automatically triggers internal dividend debt recalculations (`_updateUserDividendDebt`) for both sender and recipient before altering state balances. This prevents unauthorized rental yields drainage during secondary market handovers.

### 3. Gas-Optimized Yield Distribution Model (`claimDividends`)
* **Mechanism**: Replaced risky, loop-heavy iteration pipelines ("Push Model") with a passive **"Pull Model Ledger"**.
* **Scalability**: The contract accounts for monthly rental distribution metrics via zero-gas variables adjustments. Fractions holders claim their individual yields on-demand, enabling the contract to host millions of active shareholders without ever hitting block gas limits.
